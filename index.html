League Of Legends Galaxy

League Of Legends Galaxy is a Discord bot for the League of Legends España community server. It lets members link their League of Legends account to their Discord account.

How it works

  1. The member runs a command in Discord and enters their Riot ID.
  2. The bot asks them to equip a specific summoner icon.
  3. The bot checks the icon through the Riot API to confirm account ownership.
  4. Once verified, the member receives a verified role in the server.

Privacy policy

  • We only store the user's Discord ID and their Riot PUUID.
  • We do not collect passwords or any other sensitive data.
  • Data is used only to keep the account link inside the League of Legends España server.
  • Data is never sold or shared with third parties.
  • Any user can ask to unlink their account and have their data deleted at any time.
